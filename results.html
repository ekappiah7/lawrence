  • Conducted comprehensive market audit to identify root causes of decline
  • Rebuilt key distributor relationships with structured performance agreements
  • Launched targeted product visibility campaigns across priority outlets
  • Activated field-level promotions and consumer sampling to rebuild brand trust
  • Recruited and trained new market-level sales ambassadors
